## 1. How to read a provenance record

Each record has the twelve requested fields, using the following normalized layout rather than repeating long source passages:

1. **ID** and 2. **description** appear in the first column.
3. **Value and units**, 4. **formula**, and 8. **inputs/dimensional analysis** appear in the value/formula column. For a scalar input, formula is “given”; for categorical rules, physical units are not applicable.
5. **Classification** and approval status appear together.
6. **Exact source URL and section/page** and 7. **accurate passage summary** resolve through the source-ID catalogue below, supplemented by the row's locator. A proposal with no external numerical source explicitly says so; a contextual NASA article does not become its numerical source.
9. **Gameplay purpose** and 11. **GAMEPLAY reference** appear together.
10. **Assumptions/limitations** and 12. **verification/boundary cases** are explicit in each row. A referenced fixture is an expected acceptance test unless the validation section says it was actually executed.

The classifications are **DOCUMENTED**, **CALCULATED**, and **ASSUMED**. “DOCUMENTED game rule” means specified by GAMEPLAY, not measured by NASA. “CALCULATED; assumptions inherited” means the result depends on at least one assumed input. Unknowns remain **UNRESOLVED**, rather than acquiring a fabricated value. Existing `DOC`, `CALC`, and `ASSUMED` badges map to these terms; `SEC` evidence in NASA_ENGINEERING is not promoted to DOCUMENTED. Approximate source ratings remain approximate even when the simulation uses a fixed representative value.

Approval identifiers refer to the companion owner record: **A01** observation/storage; **A02** vehicles/sites/windows/events; **A03** landing/damage; **A04** energy semantics/initial conditions/downhill; **A05** relay/contact profiles; **A05b** O3 outage interpretation; **A06** scientific products/objectives/D12; **A08** scoring. Existing established assumptions are identified separately from new proposals requiring approval.
